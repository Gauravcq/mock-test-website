GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS

Please read the instructions carefully

  1. Total duration: The total duration of the examination has been mentioned before starting the test.
  2. Server time: The countdown timer in the top right corner displays the remaining time for you to complete the examination.
  3. Question Palette: The Question Palette displayed on the right side shows the status of each question using the following symbols:
    1 You have not visited the question yet.
    2 You have not answered the question.
    3 You have answered the question.
    4 You have NOT answered the question but marked for review.
    5 You have answered and marked for review (will be evaluated).
  4. The Marked for Review status simply indicates you would like to look at that question again.
  5. You can click on the > arrow to collapse the question palette.

Navigating to a Question:

  1. To answer a question, do the following:
    • Click on the question number in the Question Palette to go directly to that question.
    • Click on Save & Next to save your answer and move to the next question.
    • Click on Mark for Review & Next to mark for review and move to next question.

Answering Questions:

  1. Procedure for answering a multiple choice question:
    • Select your answer by clicking on one of the option buttons
    • To deselect, click on the button again or click Clear Response
    • To save your answer, click Save & Next
    • To mark for review, click Mark for Review & Next
  2. Only questions for which answers are saved will be considered for evaluation.
